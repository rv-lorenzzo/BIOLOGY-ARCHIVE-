Section 01

The strategy

Watch → revise → practice

A loop, not a checklist. Do not skip step two. Read NCERT yourself the same day you watch the lecture — the rest of the loop only works if this one holds.

  1. 01

    Watch a detailed oneshot

    Open a teacher, pick the chapter, finish the full lecture. Don't switch teachers mid-chapter.

  2. 02

    Revise NCERT yourself

    Read the chapter from NCERT right after the lecture. This is the step most students skip — and the reason the loop breaks.

  3. 03

    Practice questions

    If you have a question bank, solve it yourself. No book? Use the practice playlists in section 04.

  4. 04

    Rapid revision

    Revise from Vipin Sir's 45-minute lectures or the 10-day crash course. Then practise again.

  5. 05

    Apex 20 — traps & exceptions

    Practice the Apex 20 series for biology if you want to master the traps, exceptions, and tough, unpredictable questions — and learn how to handle them before they trap you in the exam.

  6. 06

    Day-before-exam revision

    Seep Mam's under-20-minute lectures plus the questions you marked earlier. Nothing new.

  7. 07

    Go for the exam

    You've done the loop. Trust it.
